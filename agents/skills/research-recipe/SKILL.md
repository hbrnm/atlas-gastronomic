# Research Recipe

## Purpose
Research the identity, history, variants and technical foundations of a dish.

## Workflow
1. Define the exact dish and editorial questions.
2. Build a source plan matched to historical, regional and technical claims.
3. Gather independent sources and verify the passages actually used.
4. Extract ingredients, ratios, techniques, variants and terminology.
5. Classify claims using the canonical confidence labels.
6. Document contradictions, gaps and limits of the evidence.
7. Produce a research dossier before drafting.

## Output
Provide a concise result with:
- scope and inputs;
- actions performed;
- evidence and references;
- findings and confidence;
- unresolved questions and blockers;
- exact files changed, if any;
- verification results.

## Failure conditions
Stop and report the blocker if required evidence is unavailable, source identity cannot be verified, a material contradiction remains unresolved, an approval boundary is reached, or the requested outcome would require inventing information.
