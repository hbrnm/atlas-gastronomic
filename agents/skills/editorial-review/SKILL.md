# Editorial Review

## Purpose
Review clarity, structure, tone, claims and reader usability.

## Workflow
1. Check required recipe sections against the editorial standard.
2. Remove unsupported superlatives and unqualified historical assertions.
3. Check terminology, Romanian language, units and consistency.
4. Ensure variants and substitutions are clearly distinguished.
5. Check that uncertainty and editorial interpretation are visible.
6. Return actionable findings; do not grant human approval.

## Output
Provide a concise result with:
- scope and inputs;
- actions performed;
- evidence and references;
- findings and confidence;
- unresolved questions and blockers;
- exact files changed, if any;
- verification results.

## Failure conditions
Stop and report the blocker if required evidence is unavailable, source identity cannot be verified, a material contradiction remains unresolved, an approval boundary is reached, or the requested outcome would require inventing information.
