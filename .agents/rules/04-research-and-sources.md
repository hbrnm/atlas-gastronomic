# Research and source integrity

Follow `standards/v1.0.0/docs/04-research-protocol.md`.

Match source authority to the claim. Classical culinary works are useful for canonical techniques and preparations; specialist manuals for method; credible regional archives and cultural sources for provenance; reputable contemporary chefs/publications for current practice. Blogs and forums are leads or evidence of practice, not sole authority for historical claims.

Record the source identity and available bibliographic details: author, title, publisher/site, edition, year, page/section, URL and access date as applicable. Verify the cited passage. Never fabricate missing metadata.

Compare independent sources and report material contradictions. Do not collapse regional variation into a false universal authentic version.
