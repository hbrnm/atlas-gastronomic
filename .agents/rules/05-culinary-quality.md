# Culinary quality

Follow the culinary and technical requirements in the Standards Pack.

Check ingredient identity, quantity, unit, preparation state, yield, equipment, sequence, heat, temperature, duration and observable success cues. Explain critical transformations, not merely actions.

Distinguish oven temperature, cooking-medium temperature and internal food temperature. Address food safety, allergens, cooling, storage and reheating when relevant. Do not present colour or time alone as a universal safety guarantee.

Do not use generic density assumptions for volume-to-mass conversion. Show formulas and assumptions for scaling and nutrition estimates. If critical data is absent, mark it unknown or request clarification rather than inventing it.
