# Editorial integrity

Follow `standards/v1.0.0/docs/01-editorial-standard.md` and the editorial manual.

Identify the dish, culinary tradition, region, defining characteristics and accepted variants before writing. Distinguish documented tradition from common practice and editorial adaptation.

Use the confidence labels `documented`, `widely_attested`, `editorial_interpretation` and `uncertain`. Important claims must be traceable to identified sources. If evidence is insufficient, say so.

Prioritize technical correctness and reproducibility over stylistic flourish. Use calm, precise Romanian by default unless the task specifies another language.
