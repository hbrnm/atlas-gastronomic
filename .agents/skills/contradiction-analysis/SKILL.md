# Contradiction Analysis

## Purpose
Analyze conflicts between sources, records or approved decisions.

## Workflow
1. State the exact conflicting propositions.
2. Identify source authority, independence, date and context.
3. Distinguish true contradiction from regional, temporal or terminological variation.
4. Assess what evidence would resolve the issue.
5. Present options, confidence and editorial consequences.
6. Do not silently choose a winner or overwrite an approved decision.

## Output
Provide a concise result with:
- scope and inputs;
- actions performed;
- evidence and references;
- findings and confidence;
- unresolved questions and blockers;
- exact files changed, if any;
- verification results.

## Failure conditions
Stop and report the blocker if required evidence is unavailable, source identity cannot be verified, a material contradiction remains unresolved, an approval boundary is reached, or the requested outcome would require inventing information.
