# Source Verification

## Purpose
Verify source identity, bibliographic metadata and claim support.

## Workflow
1. Confirm the source exists and is accessible or identify access limitations.
2. Verify author, title, publisher/site, edition, year and page/section where available.
3. Inspect the actual passage supporting the claim.
4. Check whether the source is primary, specialist, secondary or anecdotal.
5. Record exact claim-to-source linkage and confidence.
6. Reject invented, unverified or mismatched citations.

## Output
Provide a concise result with:
- scope and inputs;
- actions performed;
- evidence and references;
- findings and confidence;
- unresolved questions and blockers;
- exact files changed, if any;
- verification results.

## Failure conditions
Stop and report the blocker if required evidence is unavailable, source identity cannot be verified, a material contradiction remains unresolved, an approval boundary is reached, or the requested outcome would require inventing information.
