# Recipe Development

## Purpose
Develop a reproducible recipe draft from an approved research basis.

## Workflow
1. Read the research dossier and relevant standards.
2. Define dish identity, yield, equipment and intended audience.
3. Normalize ingredients, quantities, units, states and groupings.
4. Write ordered steps with actions, heat, time and observable cues.
5. Check ingredient usage, yield, timing and technical plausibility.
6. Address allergens, food safety, storage and nutrition only where supportable.
7. Keep status at draft or technical_review; never approve or publish.

## Output
Provide a concise result with:
- scope and inputs;
- actions performed;
- evidence and references;
- findings and confidence;
- unresolved questions and blockers;
- exact files changed, if any;
- verification results.

## Failure conditions
Stop and report the blocker if required evidence is unavailable, source identity cannot be verified, a material contradiction remains unresolved, an approval boundary is reached, or the requested outcome would require inventing information.
