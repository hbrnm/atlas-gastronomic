# Culinary Review

## Purpose
Review gastronomic identity, ratios, method and reproducibility.

## Workflow
1. Compare the draft with the documented dish identity and tradition.
2. Check ingredient compatibility, ratios and preparation states.
3. Check sequence, transformations, heat, temperature and timing.
4. Check equipment, yield, scaling assumptions and observable cues.
5. Check food-safety and storage instructions.
6. Return PASS, FAIL or NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW per criterion with evidence.

## Output
Provide a concise result with:
- scope and inputs;
- actions performed;
- evidence and references;
- findings and confidence;
- unresolved questions and blockers;
- exact files changed, if any;
- verification results.

## Failure conditions
Stop and report the blocker if required evidence is unavailable, source identity cannot be verified, a material contradiction remains unresolved, an approval boundary is reached, or the requested outcome would require inventing information.
