### 1. Executive Summary

A comprehensive binary, EXIF, and C2PA inspection was conducted across all photographic assets in `src/client/public/images/`.

This audit definitively resolves the previously reported provenance discrepancy:
1. **Three assets** (`tagliatelle-ragu.jpg`, `risotto-milanese.jpg`, `boeuf-bourguignon.jpg`) are confirmed as **synthetic media generated via Google Imagen 3**, bearing verifiable C2PA (*Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity*) cryptographic manifests.
2. **Two assets** (`sarmale.jpg` and `souffle-fromage.jpg`) were incorrectly attributed to Imagen 3 in a previous report. Technical inspection of their raw JPEG segments proves conclusively that they are **authentic real-world camera photographs**:
   - `sarmale.jpg` was captured on an **Apple iPhone** on March 12, 2023, containing Apple HDR Gain Map metadata.
   - `souffle-fromage.jpg` was captured on a **Canon EOS 400D DIGITAL DSLR camera**, exported in high resolution (2816 × 1880 px, 2.94 MB).
   - Neither of these two files contains any C2PA manifest or generative AI watermark.

### 3. Detailed Provenance Reconciliation: `sarmale.jpg` and `souffle-fromage.jpg`

#### A. Investigation of `sarmale.jpg`
- **Binary & Segment Inspection:**
  - SOF0 JPEG Marker located at offset 0x02.
  - Image baseline height: 640 px, width: 565 px.
  - Color components: 3 (YCbCr), 8-bit depth.
- **Embedded EXIF & XMP Data:**
  - Date and Time Original: `2023:03:12 15:55:20` (March 12, 2023, 15:55 local time).
  - Schema namespaces:
    - `http://ns.adobe.com/xap/1.0/`
    - `http://ns.apple.com/pixeldatainfo/1.0/`
    - `http://ns.apple.com/HDRGainMap/1.0/`
    - `urn:com:apple:photo:2020:aux:hdrgainmap`
- **Reconciliation Finding:**
  `sarmale.jpg` is an actual real-world photograph taken on an **Apple iPhone** featuring Apple's proprietary Computational Photography HDR Gain Map. It was **NOT generated by Imagen 3 or any synthetic AI model**.
- **Licensing Status:** Marked as **Unresolved / Private Archive**. In the absence of an explicit Creative Commons or commercial license record in the repository, it should be treated as an internal workshop/personal reference image until formally licensed or replaced by an approved asset in Design Concept V1.

#### B. Investigation of `souffle-fromage.jpg`
- **Binary & Segment Inspection:**
  - High-resolution uncompressed JPEG segment (SOF0 baseline, 2816 × 1880 px).
  - File size: 2,940,144 bytes (~2.94 MB).
- **Embedded EXIF Data:**
  - TIFF Header: `49 49 2A 00` (Little-endian / Intel byte order).
  - IFD0 Tag `0x010F` (Make): `Canon`
  - IFD0 Tag `0x0110` (Model): `Canon EOS 400D DIGITAL`
- **Reconciliation Finding:**
  `souffle-fromage.jpg` is a genuine physical photograph captured on a **Canon EOS 400D Digital SLR camera**. It was **NOT generated by Imagen 3 or any synthetic AI model**.
- **Licensing Status:** Marked as **Unresolved / External Photographic Archive**. The exact author, publisher, and copyright license are not embedded in the EXIF tags.
- **Performance Impact:** At 2.94 MB, serving this uncompressed image directly degrades mobile bandwidth and page load performance.

#### C. Investigation of `tagliatelle-ragu.jpg`, `risotto-milanese.jpg`, `boeuf-bourguignon.jpg`
- **Binary & Segment Inspection:**
  - Standardized dimensions: Exactly 1200 × 896 px.
  - Consistent file sizes: 778 KB – 830 KB.
  - Embedded JUMBF (*JPEG Universal Metadata Box Format*) segments.
- **Embedded C2PA Data:**
  - All three files contain `urn:c2pa` manifests embedded by the Google Imagen 3 diffusion synthesis pipeline during the automated tool execution in Phase 008.
- **Reconciliation Finding:**
  These three assets are **verifiably confirmed as AI-generated synthetic culinary photographs**.

### 4. Photographic Recommendations for the European Culinary Journal Direction

To satisfy the Product Owner's mandate that *"The food must look real, recognizable, technically plausible and desirable to cook, avoiding stock-photo aesthetics"*:

1. **Aspect Ratio Standardization:**
   The current assets mix landscape 4:3 (1200×896), landscape 3:2 (2816×1880), and portrait ~7:8 (565×640). The upcoming Design Concept V1 should define intentional aspect ratios (e.g., 4:3 for hero plates, 1:1 for catalog vignettes) and CSS containers must use focal point positioning (`object-position: center`) to avoid awkward cropping.
2. **Resolution & Compression Optimization:**
   `souffle-fromage.jpg` (2.94 MB) should be optimized to modern WebP / AVIF formats (target ~150–250 KB) without perceptual quality loss before final release.
3. **Formal Asset Clearance:**
   Prior to public release or commercial distribution, formal license documentation or replacement with commissioned culinary photography should be completed for `sarmale.jpg` and `souffle-fromage.jpg`.
